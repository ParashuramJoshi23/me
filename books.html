The Effective Engineer

by Edmond Lau

Helped me internalise leverage — focusing on the work that actually moves the needle — and what it means to be genuinely useful to the team I'm working in.

What I Talk About When I Talk About Running

by Haruki Murakami

Kindled my love for running. I lived through "pain is inevitable, suffering is optional" first-hand while working my way up to 10k.

Masala Lab: The Science of Indian Cooking

by Krish Ashok

Engineered cooking. I loved its pragmatic approach to the everyday stuff — how to preserve and reheat food without ruining it.

Designing Data-Intensive Applications

by Martin Kleppmann

I tried reading this multiple times and bounced off. After finishing Arpit Bhayani's system design course, everything finally clicked — and it's been one of the most rewarding technical reads since.
